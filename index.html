Performance tiers (plain English)

  • Pro M5 / M4: OLED + 120Hz. Fastest chips and best display. M5 new starts $1,199; M4 Apple Refurb 11″ 256GB $909.
  • Air M4 (current): 12 GB RAM, newest Air chip, $749 new — longevity sweet spot if budget stretches.
  • Air M3: Almost as fast as M4 for everyday use; Apple Refurb $589 / Back Market from ~$534.
  • Air M2 / Pro M2: Still Apple Intelligence; good if priced under ~$500 refurbished. Shorter remaining support than M3/M4.
  • Air M1 (5th gen): Cheapest AI‑capable Air (~$320–$360). 8 GB RAM. Best “won’t age out soon” floor for this budget. 64 GB is the common cheap config — plan iCloud or pick 256 GB when you can.
  • Base A16: New ~$399–$449. Fine for media/notes. No Apple Intelligence, 6 GB RAM — not the longevity pick for this brief.

Caveats

  • Wi‑Fi only; cellular adds roughly $150–$200.
  • Support years are planning estimates from typical iPadOS windows — not an Apple promise.
  • Prefer Apple Certified Refurbished when the price gap is small; otherwise Plug / Back Market / Best Buy refurbished with clear warranty.
  • Ignore suspiciously cheap Shopify listings (~$50) — treated as unreliable.
  • Amazon Renewed rows are from search results only — product pages did not load; confirm seller, grade, and Wi‑Fi vs cellular on the ASIN page before buying. No Renewed A16 listed.
  • Costco/Target/Walmart A16 or Air street stock was incomplete online when checked; Costco A16 128GB unavailable online.
  • Re‑open every link before buying — refurbished stock turns over fast.
